# Local scene label sheet for independent raters (fixed 2026-11-08, before any result was looked at)

Thank you for helping. This takes about 20 minutes. Please work alone, from your own knowledge, and do not look anything up.

## What we are asking
For each place, tell us how much is going on within about a 15-minute walk (three quarters of a mile) of its center: places to eat, drink, hang out and browse.

- **Quiet**: sleepy or residential, few places to go on foot. You would drive for an evening out.
- **Some**: a real but modest center. A handful of places to eat or drink, a main street, not much more.
- **Vibrant**: busy streets, lots of places in every direction, people out at most hours, an evening out without leaving the neighborhood.

This is about how much activity there is, not about quality, price, safety, who lives there, or whether the place is cool. A big chain-and-office district can be vibrant; a lovely leafy town can be quiet.

## Rules
1. Answer only places you know. In column D write **well**, **a bit**, or **no**. Leave E and F empty for a **no**. You need at least 25 places you know, or we cannot use your sheet.
2. Column E: write exactly **Quiet**, **Some**, or **Vibrant**. Go with your first sense; there is no right answer to find.
3. Column F, only for places you marked Vibrant: **yes** if it is vibrant mainly because of independent cafes, bars, restaurants and culture spots; **no** if mainly because of chains, offices, traffic or a long commercial strip; **cant say** if you cannot tell.
4. Do not look at maps, reviews or any rating. Do not talk to the other raters until all sheets are in. You will not see any score from us.
5. Send the sheet back as is (the matching CSV, `docs/local_scene_label_sheet.csv`, is the same list for a spreadsheet).
